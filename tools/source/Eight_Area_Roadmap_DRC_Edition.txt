 1. Executive summary and bottom line
  Bottom line. One plan, eight areas, all moving every week, for about 8.5 hours of study a week
  plus exercise: 45 minutes on weekday evenings, about 2 hours on Saturday and about 2¾ hours on
  Sunday.

  Python and AI builds the Go-live Data Kit: the validator finds vendor-data errors, and a coded RAG
  assistant explains how to fix them, naming its source. Milestones: 22 Nov validator update · 29 Nov
  manager update · 20 Dec 16/20 test score · 27 Dec 3-minute demo · 31 Jan extended validator and
  SQL demo.

  Accounting restarts at NPTEL lecture 1.2, 30 minutes each weekday, with a cold quiz every Monday
  and a cumulative test at every stage. By 31 Jan: journal and ledger. Then trial balance (Feb), final
  accounts (Mar), finance practice (Apr–May) and SAP S/4HANA Finance (Jun–Oct).

  The other six areas run in small, fixed slots: French 10 min Mon–Thu plus a Friday quiz;
  communication habits inside real work plus a Sunday recording; LinkedIn 15 min on Sundays; the
  evidence log 5 min a day; the tracker app in 30-minute Sunday steps; and health, family and money
  protected by daily habits and six dated actions.

  Busy go-live week? Switch to the reduced routine in section 13 (about 20 minutes a day). Follow
  the order, not the dates. A missed day moves the next task one slot later; nothing is lost.

  Python, then TF-IDF with scikit-learn; the 'answer' is the top 3 chunks with their sources. Online (only
  with a company-approved API key): embeddings and an AI model write the answer from the top 3
  chunks; keep the key in a .env file, never in your code.
  Install ahead while the connection is good: pandas, openpyxl, scikit-learn, streamlit. SQLite needs
  nothing: it's built into Python.

  Rules that don't change
  • Fictional data only for all practice.
  • Never put client names, PAN, GSTIN, bank details, invoices or confidential internal information into
    external AI tools or personal repositories, unless the company expressly approves it.
  • Type and understand code; don't copy AI output blindly. Predict the output before running it.
  • Stuck for 15 minutes? Ask for a hint, not a complete solution.
  • Keep the plan manageable during go-live periods: use section 13 without guilt.
  • Recover after missed days by continuing in order, not by doubling up.
  • Protect health, sleep and family responsibilities. No streak is worth losing them.
  • No new subjects unless this plan is reviewed first.
  • Keep the original objectives unless there's a clear, written reason to change one.
  • Private GitHub repos; app signing key only in GitHub Secrets and your private Drive.
